# Sigma Designs (family hub)

Sigma's design languages, each distilled from a shipped project into recipes
with exact numbers. Pick the child, read its SKILL.md, and follow it; do not
mix the two languages on one page.

## Route to the right child

| Need | Skill |
|---|---|
| Living gradient orbs, soap bubbles, 3–4 sliding rim lights, aurora or full-gradient atmospheres, 9-stop warm palette, dark WebGL showpiece pages, WebGL story rails, glassy synthesized UI sound, in-bubble text, gradient-edge cursors | **sigma-designs-living-glass** |
| The Akademiya AI school website or anything that must match it: B2B pages for school directors, the sky hero, "Sound familiar?" pains, the "Let's fix this" turn, real 3D phones and tilting screenshots, the old-site glass nav, uz/ru/en copy and truth rules | **akademiya-ai-school-design** |

### Tie-break when the brief is ambiguous

Both languages can use red/violet blooms on velvet, so "blooms on velvet" alone routes nowhere.

| Brief says | Route |
|---|---|
| Akademiya, schools, directors, or a light-first marketing page with one dark chapter | **akademiya-ai-school-design** |
| A full dark showpiece, orbs/bubbles, the 9-stop warm palette, a WebGL atmosphere | **sigma-designs-living-glass** |
| Still ambiguous | Ask one question: "light-first school marketing page, or a full dark living-glass showpiece?" |

## Shared across the family

- **Proof over taste.** Every recipe was verified in a browser with measured
  numbers; keep doing that (each child has a verification reference).
- **Alive, never noisy.** Motion carries meaning on multi-second periods;
  reduced motion gets a calm, complete page.
- **Real over drawn.** Real light and real product; no placeholders posing as
  the thing.

Living glass is a dark, luminous showpiece language; the school language is a
light-first marketing system that drops into velvet for one chapter. When a
brief names Akademiya, schools or directors, it is the school language.
